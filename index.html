Progressive Overload

Tells you the exercise, the weight, the plates to load, the sets and the reps — then rewrites the next set the moment you log the last one.

Open the app

Put it on your home screen

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. It appears as an app icon.

Opens fullscreen with no browser bar, and works with no signal once installed.

Note

Your training data is stored on this device only. There is no account yet, so it does not sync between phone and computer. Use Export in the Plan tab for a backup.

Must be opened in Safari on iPhone to install.
